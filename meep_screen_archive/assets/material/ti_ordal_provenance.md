The paper reports room-temperature submillimeter absorption measurements and
combines them with previous higher-frequency measurements through Kramers–Kronig
analysis. Do not call the full table direct 6–16 um measurements or assign an
unknown exact sample temperature. No high-temperature or actual-sample validation
is claimed. The fit uses PCHIP n,k versus the retained rounded wavelengths,
without extrapolation; this interpolation is a specified optical model, not
additional measured data. Original-node and interpolated-grid errors are separate.
